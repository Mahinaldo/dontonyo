## Verification convention

`verified` means an external source directly corroborates the limited proposition used in the import. `source_attributed` means a scan-derived line is retained only as dated source material and is not presented as a complete, neutral, current statement. `conflicting_source` means the scan contains a material error, ambiguity, or unsafe premise. Territorial, political, religious, institutional, demographic, security, and superlative wording remains source-attributed even where surrounding historical context can be corroborated.
