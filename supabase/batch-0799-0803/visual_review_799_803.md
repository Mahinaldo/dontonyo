## Initial Image-Grounded Classification Boundary

Pages 799–802 contain author–work tables and prior-exam questions; pages 803 contains a quotation-attribution table that itself flags misattribution risk. Political, national, leadership, military, ideological, legal, economic, religiously adjacent, current-status, translation, ISBN, source-imprecise, and already-imported material remains provenance-only. No source item is eligible for learner-facing admission unless it also clears exact visual readability, OCR reconciliation, duplicate screening, and direct authority verification.
