# External Verification Ledger — Pages 559–563

## Verification convention

`verified` means an external source directly corroborates the limited historical or descriptive proposition used in a database record. `source_attributed` means the scan is retained as dated learning-source material without representing its wording as a present-tense, neutral, complete, or independently settled statement. `conflicting_source` means the scan’s wording is materially inaccurate, overbroad, or unsafe to normalize. Political, territorial, religious, diplomatic, institutional, nuclear, and security claims retain source attribution even where historical context is corroborated.
