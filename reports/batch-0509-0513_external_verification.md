## Scope note

The data model separates source transcription from verification. A `conflicting` row is not deleted or overwritten: it remains discoverable with the scanned-book provenance, its printed answer/wording, and the reason it was not treated as a current fact. This batch therefore preserves study-source fidelity while preventing the public library from presenting contested or time-sensitive claims as unqualified truth.
