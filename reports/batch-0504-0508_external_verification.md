## Scope note

The batch does not silently normalize source-specific spellings, regional classifications, capital-role labels, or dated terms. Examples include the source’s Bolivia role labels, the “first digital currency” phrasing, and “Green Money” wording. These are explicitly retained with reviewable status rather than converted into unsupported facts.
