## Page 456–457: Khudiram Bose and `একবার বিদায় দে মা ঘুরে আসি`

The source pages connect Khudiram Bose’s execution with the song `একবার বিদায় দে মা ঘুরে আসি` and attribute it to `পীতাম্বর সেন`. External sources corroborate the execution date as **11 August 1908**, but do **not** support the source page’s exact attribution.

The Bengali Wikipedia article attributes the song to the Bankura folk poet **পীতাম্বর দাস**, says it was written and composed in his honour after Khudiram’s execution, and gives the execution date as 11 August 1908. It cites published and government-cultural sources, but remains a tertiary source. [Bengali Wikipedia](https://bn.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%A6%8F%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0_%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%A6%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%BC_%E0%A6%A6%E0%A7%87_%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BE_%E0%A6%98%E0%A7%81%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%87_%E0%A6%86%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%BF)

Bangladesh’s Teachers’ Portal separately states that the execution was carried out on 11 August 1908 and identifies **পীতাম্বর দাস** as the folk poet who wrote the song. The portal article is teacher-authored content hosted by a government-supported education platform, so it is corroborative rather than a primary historical source. [Teachers’ Portal](https://www.teachers.gov.bd/blog/details/736394?page=1614&gt-11i-agsht-1908sale-kshudiramer-fannsi-karzkr-hy)

RisingBD also gives 11 August 1908, but explicitly reports an attribution dispute: some accounts name **পীতাম্বর দাস**, while others name **মুকুন্দ দাস**. [RisingBD](https://www.risingbd.com/%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B7%E0%A7%81%E0%A6%A6%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AE%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0-%E0%A6%AB%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%81%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%B0-%E0%A7%A7%E0%A7%A8%E0%A7%AA%E0%A6%A4%E0%A6%AE-%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B7%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%80/469236)

**Import decision:** preserve the book’s original wording as a source excerpt and use a low-confidence, source-attributed note. Do not store `পীতাম্বর সেন` as a verified author. Add a verification flag noting that external sources point to `পীতাম্বর দাস` and that a secondary-source attribution dispute exists.

[Museo Reina Sofía collection record](https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/collections/artwork/guernica-0) [Rethinking Guernica research site](https://guernica.museoreinasofia.es/en)

Preserve the printed wording in `source_excerpt` and record this normalization in the audit ledger.

## Page 457: Statue of Liberty

The U.S. National Park Service verifies that the Statue of Liberty was proposed as a French gift to mark the United States’ independence centennial and the friendship between France and the United States; it was officially unveiled on **28 October 1886**. [Creating the Statue of Liberty](https://www.nps.gov/stli/learn/historyculture/places_creating_statue.htm) [NPS facts](https://www.nps.gov/stli/learn/statue-of-liberty-facts.htm)

**Import decision:** retain the core claim as high-confidence historical content, but normalize the ambiguous printed phrase `ফ্রান্স ১৮৮৬ সালে আমেরিকাকে উপহার পাঠায়` to **dedicated/unveiled in 1886**. The NPS chronology says the statue arrived in New York in 1885 and was unveiled in 1886, so `sent in 1886` should not be presented as the verified historical wording. Preserve the original printed claim in the source excerpt and flag the distinction in the audit record.
