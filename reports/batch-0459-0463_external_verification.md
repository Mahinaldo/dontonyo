## Page 463: Bangladesh Television and telecommunications

Bangladesh Television’s official history confirms its pilot launch on **25 December 1964**, operation from two rooms in the former **DIT Building** (now the Rajuk office), the **1975** move to Rampura, and the start of colour broadcasting in **1980**. [Bangladesh Television](http://btv.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922db5a933eb65569e099bf)

The Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission’s historical timeline contradicts two printed page-463 dates: it records **card telephone service in 1995**, not 1992, and digital telecom milestones in **1981** and **1983**, rather than a blanket first digital telephone system in 1990. It also identifies BTRC as the regulator established under the 2001 Telecommunications Act, with operations beginning in 2002. [BTRC telecom-history timeline](http://btrc.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922e040933eb65569e26312)

**Import decision:** mark the BTV launch, DIT/Rajuk, Rampura, and colour-broadcasting facts as **verified**. Mark the printed 1992 card-phone and 1990 first-digital-telephone statements as **conflicting** with the primary regulator timeline; preserve their printed wording, flag them for study caution, and do not silently normalize or erase them. Other telecommunications and Bangladesh Betar claims remain **source-attributed** pending direct corroboration.
