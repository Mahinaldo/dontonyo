# Batch 0469–0473 External Verification Ledger

## Page 470: armed forces

Bangladesh Air Force’s official history identifies **A. K. Khandker** as its first Chief of Air Staff and describes the 1971 Air Wing at Dimapur. [Bangladesh Air Force — History](https://baf.mil.bd/baf-history.php) The Osmani Museum, a branch of the Bangladesh National Museum, records that **M. A. G. Osmani** was Commander-in-Chief of Bangladesh Forces from 12 April 1971 to 7 April 1972 and was regarded as a founder of Bangladesh’s armed forces. [Osmani Museum — About Us](https://www.osmanimuseum.org.bd/e/about-us/) Prothom Alo, citing an ISPR handout, identifies **Captain Nurul Huq** as the first Chief of Naval Staff from 1972 to 1973. [Prothom Alo — Nurul Huq](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/bangladeshs-first-chief-of-naval-staff-navy-nurul-huq-dies)

**Import decision:** mark only those directly supported first-chief propositions as **verified**. Retain the book’s exact commencement dates, headquarters, symbols, slogans, and other service details as source-attributed until a matching source is retained.

## Page 473: named operations

The U.S. Marine Corps’ official history identifies **Operation Sea Angel** as the May–June 1991 humanitarian operation in Bangladesh after Cyclone Marian, and identifies **Operation Sea Angel II** as the November–December 2007 Bangladesh response to Cyclone Sidr. [U.S. Marine Corps University — Humanitarian Operations](https://www.usmcu.edu/Research/Marine-Corps-History-Division/Information-for-Units/-Marine-Corps-Humanitarian-Operations/) A 2007 11th Marine Expeditionary Unit record documents Sea Angel II relief aid after Sidr. [11th MEU — Sea Angel II](https://www.11thmeu.marines.mil/Media-Room/Stories/Article/Article/533488/sea-angel-ii-lands-in-bangladesh-with-more-aid/) The U.S. Marine Corps’ historical monograph documents the 1991 Bangladesh relief effort. [U.S. Marine Corps — *Angels from the Sea*](https://www.marines.mil/Portals/1/Publications/ANGELS%20FROM%20THE%20SEA%20-%20RELIEF%20OPERATIONS%20IN%20BANGLADESH%201991%20PCN%2019000316400_1.pdf)

The Daily Star’s contemporaneous report, citing an ISPR briefing, identifies **Operation Thunderbolt** as the Bangladesh Army operation at Holey Artisan Bakery on 2 July 2016. [The Daily Star — Operation Thunderbolt](https://www.thedailystar.net/city/gulshan-attack-ispr-briefing-1230pm-1249231)

**Import decision:** mark the Sea Angel, Sea Angel II, and Thunderbolt propositions as **verified**. Retain Clean Heart, Nobojatra, Dalbhat, Rebel Hunt, Devil Hunt, and Manna as source-attributed unless a matching external source is retained for the precise printed statement.
